# Requirement Quality Gates

## Quality criteria
- Clarity: understandable without extra debate
- Consistency: no contradictions across artifacts
- Feasibility: achievable inside constraints
- Testability: can be verified objectively
- Traceability: linked to objective or source
- Completeness: no critical missing facts or decisions

## Review checklist
- Is the statement precise and unambiguous?
- Does it state who benefits and why?
- Is the level of abstraction correct?
- Are assumptions, constraints, and risks distinguished?
- Are dependencies and sequencing visible?
- Are acceptance criteria concrete?

## Quality gate outcomes
- `Ready`: acceptable for planning or approval
- `Needs refinement`: missing details but directionally sound
- `Blocked`: missing critical fact or decision
- `Rejected`: not in scope or not supported by evidence
