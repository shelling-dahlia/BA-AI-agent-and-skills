# Skill: Estimation Support with Assumptions and Uncertainty

## Purpose
Support rough estimation while making assumptions, uncertainty, risk, and constraints visible.

## Input
- backlog item or task breakdown
- known complexity, scope, and dependencies
- historic team data or analogous items

## Output
- estimate ranges or relative sizing
- assumptions and uncertainty log
- risk notes
- estimate rationale

## Execution steps
1. Define the objective and boundaries of the item.
2. Identify assumptions affecting complexity.
3. Compare to similar work or reference stories.
4. Estimate with a range or relative unit and justify the basis.
5. Capture uncertainty and re-estimation triggers.

## Rules
- Estimates are not requirements and should be revisited as evidence changes.
- Uncertainty and missing information must be documented clearly.
- Do not hide dependencies behind a single number.

## Validation checks
- Are estimates traceable to assumptions?
- Are the unknowns and risks listed?
- Is it clear when the estimate should be re-evaluated?
