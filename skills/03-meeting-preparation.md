# Skill: Meeting Preparation, Notes, Decisions, and Action Tracking

## Purpose
Make meetings reusable, traceable, and actionable for BA work.

## Input
- meeting goals
- participants and status
- agenda or raw notes

## Output
- meeting notes
- decisions log
- open questions
- assumptions and risks
- action items and owners

## Execution steps
1. Establish purpose, participants, and decision agenda.
2. Record decisions separately from discussion.
3. Capture open questions, unresolved conflicts, and assumptions.
4. Link action items to owner, due date, and source requirement.
5. Update requirements and backlog artifacts after the meeting.

## Rules
- Do not mix decisions, opinions, and status updates in one list.
- Keep each action item traceable to an objective or requirement.
- Preserve the source of the decision when possible.

## Validation checks
- Are decisions actionable and timestamped?
- Are actions assigned and linked to follow-up artifacts?
- Are unresolved questions still visible and tracked?
