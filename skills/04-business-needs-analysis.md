# Skill: Business Needs, Stakeholder Analysis, Scope, and Objectives

## Purpose
Translate a raw request into business intent, stakeholder needs, outcomes, and scope boundaries.

## Input
- problem statement or opportunity
- stakeholder notes
- constraints and regulations

## Output
- business objectives
- stakeholder register
- scope in/out statements
- value hypotheses and measurable outcomes when available

## Execution steps
1. Identify the initiative objective and desired outcome.
2. Map stakeholders and their value or concern.
3. Distinguish must-have, nice-to-have, and out-of-scope items.
4. Define non-functional constraints and business rules that shape scope.
5. Capture uncertainty as an open question or assumption.

## Rules
- Business objectives explain why the change matters.
- Scope defines what is in and out for the initiative.
- Stakeholders may be customers, users, operators, regulators, or internal sponsors.

## Validation checks
- Is the goal specific enough to guide feature and backlog decisions?
- Are stakeholder needs linked to the value being pursued?
- Are assumptions and constraints clearly separated?
