# Skill: Epic Creation and Decomposition

## Purpose
Group related work into larger, value-oriented units that can be sequenced across releases or iterations.

## Input
- business goals and feature catalogue
- prioritized areas of value
- known milestone or release assumptions

## Output
- epic list
- epic descriptions
- delivery horizon and dependencies
- links to features and stories

## Execution steps
1. Identify the business outcome or initiative theme.
2. Group work by release or strategic theme.
3. Define epic objective and success measures if known.
4. Decompose into stories or use cases only when sufficient detail exists.
5. Capture dependencies and sequencing assumptions.

## Rules
- Epic is larger than a story and usually spans multiple iterations.
- An epic should not be a disguised task list.
- Epic boundaries should support sequencing, planning, and value delivery.

## Validation checks
- Is the epic user- or value-centric?
- Can it be broken into smaller, more concrete units?
- Is it aligned with a business objective and release strategy?
