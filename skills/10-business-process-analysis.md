# Skill: Business Process Analysis and Modelling

## Purpose
Understand how work is performed and where a future solution changes the process or introduces new decisions.

## Input
- business workflow notes
- actor interactions
- pain points or process gaps

## Output
- process map or narrative
- step-by-step flow
- decision points and handoffs
- problem and improvement opportunities

## Execution steps
1. Identify actors, triggers, inputs, outputs, and outcomes.
2. Capture the current or target process flow.
3. Note decision points, exceptions, and hand-offs.
4. Translate process observations into requirement or story candidates.
5. Validate that the process view aligns to the objective.

## Rules
- Do not confuse a process model with an internal design or architecture model.
- Keep process steps at the right level of abstraction.
- Distinguish current-state, target-state, and pain-point analysis.

## Validation checks
- Are the roles and steps clear?
- Does the process map trace back to a real business need?
- Are exceptions and decision points captured?
