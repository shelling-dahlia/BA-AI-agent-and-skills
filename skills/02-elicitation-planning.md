# Skill: Elicitation Planning and Question Generation

## Purpose
Prepare an elicitation plan and generate precise questions that reduce ambiguity before requirements are confirmed.

## Input
- raw requirement or backlog
- known stakeholder context
- known domain and constraints

## Output
- elicitation plan
- list of questions grouped by theme
- stakeholder mapping and interview plan
- decision and missing-information register

## Execution steps
1. Determine objectives of the elicitation session.
2. Identify key stakeholders and decision-makers.
3. Group questions by business, functional, non-functional, UX, compliance, domain, and operational topics.
4. Prioritize questions by impact and uncertainty.
5. Phrase questions to distinguish fact, preference, assumption, and constraint.
6. Capture the expected evidence to answer each question.

## Rules
- Ask open questions before closed questions.
- Separate what is known from what is assumed.
- Keep questions tied to business value or obligation.
- Capture not only what users want, but why it matters.

## Validation checks
- Are questions specific and answerable?
- Do questions reduce uncertainty rather than create more noise?
- Are decisions, assumptions, and open items captured separately?

## Example
For e-commerce: ask which buyer journeys matter first, what products are sold, what payment methods are allowed, and what must be available for launch versus later phases.
