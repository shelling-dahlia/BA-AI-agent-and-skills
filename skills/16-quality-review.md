# Skill: Requirements Quality Review, Ambiguity Detection, and Completeness Validation

## Purpose
Review requirements for quality, clarity, consistency, completeness, and readiness.

## Input
- requirement drafts or backlog items
- relevant notes, constraints, or review comments

## Output
- quality review report
- ambiguity, risk, and missing-information register
- recommendation for refinement or acceptance

## Execution steps
1. Check clarity, completeness, consistency, feasibility, and testability.
2. Flag vague terms, contradictions, missing actors, and undefined metrics.
3. Assess whether the item is at the right level of abstraction.
4. Recommend whether to refine, split, or reject the item for now.

## Rules
- Quality is not only about grammar; it is about fit, evidence, and testability.
- If an item cannot be validated, it is not ready as a confirmed requirement.

## Validation checks
- Can the requirement be read, tested, and traced?
- Are assumptions and open questions explicit?
- Is there any conflicting or duplicative wording?
