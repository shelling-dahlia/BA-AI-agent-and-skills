# Skill: SDLC Phase Mapping and Deliverable Planning

## Purpose
Map business-analysis outputs to the appropriate SDLC stage and reuse artifacts correctly across planning, build, and validation.

## Input
- project stage or methodology
- backlog and requirements set
- release or milestone information

## Output
- phase-to-artifact mapping
- deliverable list by lifecycle stage
- quality gate expectations

## Execution steps
1. Identify the current phase: discovery, planning, build, validation, release, or maintenance.
2. Match artifact types to the phase and expected decision.
3. Clarify who owns the artifact and what completion looks like.
4. Identify review and signoff activities.

## Rules
- Not every artifact is needed in every phase.
- Do not treat a story as a substitute for a formal acceptance or business rule artifact when the governance context requires it.

## Validation checks
- Are required deliverables aligned with the project phase?
- Are review gates and decision owners defined?
