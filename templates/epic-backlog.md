# Epic and User Story Backlog

## Purpose
Capture value-oriented backlog items and their delivery sequence.

## Recommended fields
- `Backlog ID`
- `Type` (`Epic`, `Story`, `Task`)
- `Title`
- `Objective`
- `Priority`
- `Status`
- `Parent item`
- `Dependent item`
- `Acceptance criteria link`
- `Owner`
