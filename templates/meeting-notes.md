# Meeting Notes and Decisions Log

## Purpose
Capture decisions, action items, open questions, and assumptions from a meeting.

## Recommended fields
- `Meeting ID`
- `Date`
- `Participants`
- `Objective`
- `Decision`
- `Reason`
- `Open question`
- `Assumption`
- `Risk`
- `Action item`
- `Owner`
- `Due date`
- `Status`
